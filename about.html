"The more things change, the more they stay the same." Jean-Baptiste Alphonse Karr, 1849

I'm building local AI systems and writing about agentic research. My current focus is Project Arrival — a multi-module research series exploring what it takes to own your agent: hardware, models, agentic tooling, and the path from Senior Data Scientist to AI Researcher.

I came to AI the long way around. Bachelor's in Environmental Science from Colby College, a self-taught obsession with computers from every angle, and several years as a Senior Data Scientist applying ML in production. Recent work: dual-RTX 3090 inference rigs running the Hermes Agent locally, open-source benchmarks, and shipped writeups documenting every decision.

Favorite things to work on right now: agentic AI, local LLM infrastructure, hardware builds, bitcoin, and thinking about brain emulation (ASI/AGI).

When I'm not programming, I'm surfing, reading, or tinkering. Follow along at blog.zacharycangemi.com or at my GitHub.
