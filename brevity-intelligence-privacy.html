Brevity Intelligence Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Brevity Intelligence is a private, noncommercial, single-user research project operated by Zachary Cangemi. It uses read-only, user-authorized access to create personal health and fitness summaries with an AI model running on privately controlled local hardware. Brevity Intelligence is not offered to the public and does not accept public registrations.

Information accessed

Brevity Intelligence may access the following categories after explicit authorization:

  • Sleep duration, timing, stages, efficiency, consistency, and performance
  • Recovery score, heart-rate variability, and resting heart rate
  • Physiological-cycle strain and heart-rate summaries
  • Workout type, timing, strain, and heart-rate summaries

Brevity Intelligence does not request profile or body-measurement access in its initial version.

How information is used

The information is used only to generate private morning briefings, personal comparisons, trend summaries, and follow-up answers for the author. It is not used for advertising, insurance, employment decisions, sale, or profiling of other people. Brevity Intelligence does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

Local AI processing and storage

Health information is processed by a self-hosted language model on privately controlled hardware. It is not submitted to ChatGPT, Claude, or another hosted language-model provider. Credentials and private records are excluded from the public source repository. Public demonstrations use synthetic or redacted data.

Local records are retained only as needed for the owner's personal research and trend analysis. The owner may delete the local records at any time.

Sharing and message delivery

Brevity Intelligence does not sell personal information. At the owner's explicit direction, a generated briefing may be delivered to the owner's personal messaging account. Only the information selected for that message is transmitted to the messaging provider; raw API responses, account credentials, and the private historical database are not transmitted.

Security

Access is read-only and limited to the scopes required by the project. Credentials are kept out of source control, private records are stored on access-controlled local systems, and network requests use encrypted transport.

Authorization and deletion

The owner can revoke the integration through the connected account provider and can delete locally retained records. Because Brevity Intelligence has no other users, requests concerning access, correction, deletion, or the project can be sent directly to the operator.
